About Area Design Elements
Name
Easter eggs of world lore. Be specific; avoid flavorless options like 'Caves', or 'Mines'. Also, Area Number & Depth Level.
Description
What becomes obvious to the players as they explore. Seed clues as to what the Area's Hidden Feature type might be, so the Caller can guess at it.
Landmark Feature
What's casually obvious at a glance. Simple and sensory.
Hidden Feature
What the players find by searching, spending time: information, interactive elements, or items. Hidden features should usually be beneficial.
Secret Feature
What Players may never find. Place one in about 3:6 rooms. Because players may be locked out of this info, never place anything here that is necessary to their progress.
Encounters
If any. How empty is your Undercrawl? Forsaken is 1:6 rooms, Forgotten is 1:12, Lost is 1:20.
Adjacent
List the adjacent Areas, their Landmark Features, and whether they are up, down, or even with the current Area. (This section is helpful but not necessary; it is repeated from the map and other Area cards.)

Fun Fact! → Landmark, Hidden, Secret is a term of design originally coined by Anne of the DIY & Dragons blog!
